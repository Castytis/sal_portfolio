About

A volunteer-run animal rescue organization focused on connecting potential adopters in the Netherlands with rescue pets from Lithuanian shelters. Through targeted social media campaigns and digital storytelling practices, the organization aims to raise awareness of stray and rescue animals in Lithuania, shares their individual stories and personalities, and assists in helping them find loving, responsible homes while promoting the importance of animal adoption.
